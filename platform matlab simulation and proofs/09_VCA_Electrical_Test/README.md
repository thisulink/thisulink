# Experiment 09: Voice Coil Actuator (VCA) Electromechanical Characterization

## 1. Electromechanical Architecture
The **THISULINK** platform utilizes a miniature linear Voice Coil Actuator (VCA) driven by a dual-H-bridge PWM driver (Texas Instruments DRV8833 / MAX22200). Unlike rotary eccentric mass (ERM) motors that suffer from frequency-amplitude coupling, a VCA provides independent control of frequency ($10 - 300\text{ Hz}$) and dynamic force amplitude ($100\text{ mN}$).

---

## 2. Governing Equations
The electrical impedance of the voice coil is:
$$Z(\omega) = R + j\omega L \implies |Z(\omega)| = \sqrt{R^2 + (\omega L)^2}$$

The current drawn under peak drive voltage $V_0 = 1.20\text{ V}$ is:
$$I_{\text{peak}}(\omega) = \frac{V_0}{|Z(\omega)|}, \quad I_{\text{rms}}(\omega) = \frac{I_{\text{peak}}}{\sqrt{2}}$$

The dynamic Lorentz force generated by the coil is:
$$F_{\text{peak}}(\omega) = K_f \cdot I_{\text{peak}}(\omega)$$

The average electrical power dissipation is:
$$P_{\text{elec}} = I_{\text{rms}}^2 \cdot R$$

---

## 3. Results Summary
- **At $50.0\text{ Hz}$ Nominal Center**:
  - $|Z| = \mathbf{8.21\ \Omega}$
  - Peak Current: $\mathbf{146.2\text{ mA}}$ ($I_{\text{rms}} \approx 103.4\text{ mA}$)
  - Output Force: $\mathbf{350.8\text{ mN}}$ peak capacity (attenuated digitally to calibrated $100\text{ mN}$)
  - Power Dissipation: $\mathbf{87.7\text{ mW}}$ ($\approx 0.088\text{ W}$)
- **Battery Autonomy**: Consuming less than $90\text{ mW}$ during an active $3\text{-second}$ sweep, a standard $3.7\text{ V}, 2500\text{ mAh}$ Li-ion battery can perform over **$5,000$ consecutive patient triage scans** on a single charge.
